# Madrona V2 visual asset brief

## Shared art direction

Create a cohesive set of warm, editorial product visuals for Madrona Product Studio.

- Calm Pacific Northwest character
- Warm off-white and natural paper surfaces
- Deep forest green, muted bark orange, charcoal, and restrained sand tones
- Soft natural daylight
- Crisp, legible interface details
- Subtle shadows only
- No gradients, glass effects, neon, generic AI imagery, stock-office scenes, or floating 3D objects
- No text baked into imagery unless the exact copy is provided below
- Leave useful negative space around the main object for responsive cropping

Export raster imagery as WebP or PNG at 2x size. Do not create fake photographs of real client work.

## Priority 1: offering module visuals

### Brand and web

Create a landscape editorial flat-lay for a fictional farm brand named “Berry Good Berry Farm.” Show a compact brand guide, berry box packaging, a simple mobile storefront, a small card, and one restrained color palette. The identity should feel established, local, generous, and practical rather than precious. Warm natural tabletop, soft daylight, forest green and berry red accents.

- Canvas: 1600 × 1200
- Safe crop: keep primary objects within the central 70%
- Label: fictional demonstration business

### Customers and growth

Create a clean product-experience composition showing a mobile commerce or membership journey for a thoughtful local food business. Include one phone with a product detail or reorder screen, one smaller confirmation surface, and one restrained metric card. The point is an easier path from discovery to repeat purchase.

- Canvas: 1600 × 1200
- Avoid unsupported claims. Use “Illustrative metric” if a number is visible.
- Keep interface copy minimal and readable.

### Operations and AI

Create a practical workflow interface, not AI-themed artwork. Show an order-intake assistant receiving a plain-language wholesale request, extracting customer, product, quantity, and delivery date, checking availability, and preparing a draft order for human review. Use a clear desktop card composition with a small human avatar and explicit review status.

- Canvas: 1600 × 1200
- Use monospace only for structured output.
- No robots, brains, sparkles, chat bubbles floating in space, or futuristic control rooms.

## Priority 2: five process images

Create five coordinated horizontal still-life or interface panels. Each should work independently at a small size.

1. **Direction**: annotated strategy brief, pencil, and simple priority map.
2. **Identity**: compact brand guide, typography specimen, and two applications.
3. **Prototype**: wireframes beside a polished mobile screen.
4. **Build**: code editor beside a responsive product interface, with code kept abstract and illegible.
5. **Stewardship**: small operating dashboard, release notes, and customer feedback summary.

- Canvas: 1200 × 800 each
- Same light, surface, palette, lens, and visual scale across all five.
- Compose for a wide 3:2 crop.

## Priority 3: icon family

Create nine single-weight line icons:

1. Farms and food
2. Outdoor and travel
3. Health and wellness
4. Shops and services
5. Brand and web
6. Customers and growth
7. Operations and AI
8. Direct partnership
9. Small senior team

Style:

- Simple, recognizable silhouettes
- Rounded line caps and joins
- No fill
- Consistent 1.75–2px optical stroke at 48px
- Deep forest linework on transparent background
- No enclosing circle in the asset. The website supplies the circle.
- Deliver SVG where possible, plus transparent PNG previews.

## Priority 4: studio photograph

The final studio image should be a real photograph, not generated.

- Charlie in a natural working or collaborative moment
- Bellingham or recognizably Pacific Northwest setting
- Daylight, candid, quiet, credible
- One or two people maximum
- Avoid staged laptop-pointing, corporate meeting rooms, or coffee-shop stock-photo energy
- Landscape crop, minimum 2400px wide

## Assets that should remain real

Do not generate replacements for:

- Lila Trips product screenshots
- San Juan Boating Guide product screenshots
- Madrona logo
- Charlie’s portrait or studio photograph

Use current product captures until refreshed screenshots are available.
