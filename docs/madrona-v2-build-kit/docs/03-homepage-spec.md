# Homepage specification

## Header

Logo left, Work / Services / Process / About in the center or right, and a compact “Let’s connect” CTA. Do not add Insights until real published content exists.

## Hero

Split composition. Copy on the left and a large organic coastal or working photograph on the right. Avoid text over a busy photograph.

## Audience strip

Four compact contexts: farms and food, outdoor and travel, health and wellness, shops and services. Use imagery or a consistent restrained icon set.

## Service section

Three connected service areas. Each includes a useful miniature artifact, not a generic illustration. Selecting an area should update the explanation and the Berry Good focus.

## Berry Good

The primary consulting demonstration. Show one business from brand through customer experience to operations. Clearly disclose that it is a demonstration business.

## Products we build and run

Feature Lila Trips and San Juan Boating Guide. Explain what each proves. Do not frame them as recommendations or products the visitor should buy.

## Studio model

A real or clearly temporary image, direct founder-led language, 15+ years, REI / Healthline / Microsoft, and strategy through working software.

## Capability sequence

Show five stages with artifacts: strategy, brand, prototype, build, and AI/automation.

## Conversion

Two paths: book a conversation, or share something already in progress.

## Footer

Dark forest. Only real links and destinations.
